Campus Brainwave Bash

Think Fast. Team Up. Take the Lead.
UTB English Club · University of Technology Bahrain
1

How to Play

Inspired by Family Feud!

Guess the most popular survey answers.

Answers are hidden on the board.

Earn points by guessing correctly!

2

Teams & Qualifiers

10 teams, 6 players each.

5 qualifier matches.

2 teams compete per match.

2 questions per match.

3

The Face-Off

1 player from each team comes forward.

Host reads the question.

First player to press the buzzer gets to answer.

Correct answer on the board = Team gains control!

4

Playing the Round

The controlling team guesses the remaining answers.

Wrong answer = 1 strike.

3 strikes = Opposing team gets a chance to steal!

Correct steal = Opponent takes the points.

Failed steal = Original team keeps the points.

5

Scoring System

Question 1: Normal Points

Question 2: Double Points

6

Road to the Semi-Finals

Top 3 teams qualify directly.

4th and 5th place compete in the Wildcard Match.

Wildcard winner claims the final semi-final spot.

7

Semi-Finals & Grand Final

4 teams compete in the semi-finals.

Top 2 teams advance to the Grand Final.

Grand Final Scoring:

Round 1: Normal Points · Round 2: Double Points

Round 3: Triple Points

Comeback opportunity! Every round counts!

8

Audience Fast Money

Your chance to win, too!

3 Audience Fast Money rounds.

1 volunteer per round.

Answer 5 quick questions against the clock.

Score 100 points or more to win a prize!

9

Audience Etiquette

Cheer for your favourite teams!

Keep the energy up!

Don't shout out answers during gameplay.

Give every team a fair chance.

10

LET THE GAMES BEGIN!
